Questions

Before you ask.

Where exactly is Terrace 35?
Lanco Hills Road, beside Lanco Hills, Khajaguda, Hyderabad 500089 — directly behind the Lanco Hills landmark towers. The Financial District at Nanakramguda is about 3 km, Gachibowli 4 to 6 km, and the ORR interchange roughly 3 km. Open the location in Google Maps.
What does a home actually cost, all in?
A 2,385 sft east-facing home starts at ₹2.62 Cr all in (calculated at ₹11,000 per sft). Across the 3 BHK corner mix, prices range up to ₹3.42 Cr for the 3,105 sft homes, while Sky Duplexes (4,770 to 6,210 sft) start at ₹5.72 Cr (calculated at ₹12,000 per sft). All figures include GST; stamp duty and registration are extra.
How many homes, and how many are left?
280 homes in one 35 floor tower, with 8 homes on a typical floor and a limited collection of Sky Duplexes on floors 32, 34 and 35. Live availability changes weekly — ask the sales desk on WhatsApp for the current inventory board.
What about parking?
Four to six covered car parks per home, depending on size — six for the 3,000 and 3,105 sft homes, four for the 2,385 to 2,625 sft homes. Parking sits inside the amenities bundle, so the higher count does not change your price. There are two basement and four podium parking levels, plus car-wash bays.
When is possession, and how do I pay?
December 2029, on a construction-linked plan across 15 milestones — 5% at booking, 15% within 15 days, then instalments tied to slabs from the basements up to the 35th floor, with the balance at registration.
Is the project RERA registered?
Telangana RERA P02400011009. The building approval number is 004197/BP/HMDA/1261/SKP/2025. Both are verifiable at rera.telangana.gov.in.
